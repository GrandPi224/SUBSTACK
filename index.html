Sep 27, 2026 AI Jobocalypse or Jobtopia

Harvard Business School

The admit rate of about 11 percent is U.S. News, as reported by mbaMission; Harvard does not publish it. The school's Class of 2027 profile lists 9,409 applications and 943 enrolled students.

Among graduates seeking jobs, 5 percent had no offer three months after graduation in 2022 (Poets&Quants), 15 percent in 2024 (Harvard Crimson) and 10 percent in 2025. Offer rates rebounded in 2025 and median base pay hit a record $185,000 (Poets&Quants, Fortune).

The founder share by class, 8 percent in 2021 to 17 percent in 2025, is from the same employment reports.

We Have Been Here Before

India's extreme poverty rate fell from 27.1 percent in 2011-12 to 5.3 percent in 2022-23, about 269 million people, at the $3.00 a day line (World Bank Poverty and Equity Brief).

Close to 800 million people in China left extreme poverty after 1978 (World Bank, Four Decades of Poverty Reduction in China, 2022).

Farm work fell from 41 percent of the U.S. workforce in 1900 to 1.9 percent in 2000 (USDA Economic Research Service, The 20th Century Transformation of U.S. Agriculture and Farm Policy).

Output per person, in 2011 dollars, from the Maddison Project Database 2023: United States $8,038 in 1900, $10,695 in 1930, $45,886 in 2000 and $58,487 in 2022; Britain $3,397 in 1811 and $7,594 in 1900. Output per person is used here as the standard measure of living standards; it is not the same as wages.

Keynes coined "technological unemployment" and predicted living standards four to eight times higher within a hundred years in Economic Possibilities for Our Grandchildren, 1930. The Luddite movement began in 1811.

Superintelligence, the word

The Oxford English Dictionary traces the word to 1822, in Richard Harrison Black's vocabulary book, a guide to word origins for students. There, superintelligence sits in a list beside animals and men: a higher mind above both, with no machine in sight. In 1965, I. J. Good described the ultraintelligent machine. In 1998, Nick Bostrom published How Long Before Superintelligence?, and in 2014 the book Superintelligence: Paths, Dangers, Strategies. In 2024, Ilya Sutskever founded Safe Superintelligence.

Jasper and Boss Mode

Jasper is an AI writing assistant. It launched in January 2021 as Conversion.ai, became Jarvis in mid-2021, and took the name Jasper in January 2022 after a trademark warning from Marvel. That put it in paying customers' hands nearly two years before ChatGPT. It ran on OpenAI's GPT-3 large language model; Jasper built the app, OpenAI built the model. Rivals included Copy.ai, Writesonic and Rytr, all built on GPT-3. Boss Mode, launched June 29, 2021, was its top plan, about $119 a month for unlimited words.

The Second Derivative

Musk's post appeared on X on September 24, 2026, about SpaceX's AI effort, then three years old against six for Anthropic and ten for OpenAI. METR's Time Horizon 1.1, published January 29, 2026, measures the length of software task a model can finish on its own at a 50 percent success rate. Across 2019 to 2025 that length doubled about every seven months. Since 2023 the doubling time is about 131 days, close to four months. By February 2026 the leading model's horizon was about 14.5 hours, most of a skilled engineer's working day. METR notes the confidence intervals are wide.

The PhD baseline of 69.7 percent on GPQA Diamond is from Epoch AI. Model scores: Gemini 3.1 Pro 94.3 percent, GPT-5.2 about 92.4 percent, Claude Opus 4.6 91.3 percent as of February 2026, and GPT-4 about 39 percent in November 2023, per an industry compilation. The analog clock finding, about 50 percent for models versus 90 percent for people, is from the Stanford AI Index 2026. Gemini Deep Think's gold-medal score at the 2025 International Math Olympiad is from Google DeepMind.

Our Wall Street cast of characters, from chipcorley.substack.com. The bull builds. The bear bellyaches. The hog binges. The owl benchmarks. The honey badger battles.

Bucky DaBull

The Bull · The optimist

The bull builds.

The bull market in the flesh. When Bucky runs, stocks climb, businesses hire, paychecks grow, and living standards rise.

The lead: our stock market archetype. The whole cast is named for him.

BitMe DaBear

The Bear · The pessimist

The bear bellyaches.

The bear market. When he takes over, stocks fall 20 percent or more from their peak, the economy often slides into recession, and job losses mount.

The antagonist: the bear market that takes the story into recession.

Hooter

The Owl · The pragmatist

The owl benchmarks.

Scale in one wing, notebook in the other. He reads the data, checks it against history, and waits for the numbers before he moves.

The mentor: he helps the hero decide.

BadAsk

The Honey Badger · The practicalist

The honey badger battles.

He asks questions, lots of them. First he asks it. Then he tasks it. Checklist in one paw, wrench in the other, he tries, fails, fixes, and tries again. Where others argue, he gets to work.

The hero’s stand-in: he shows the reader how it’s done.

Boss Hog

The Hog · The plunger

The hog binges.

Wall Street’s old word for a trader who goes all in on borrowed cash. IOUs in one hand, casino chips in the other. When the market turns, the leverage wipes him out. Bulls make money, bears make money, hogs get slaughtered.

The tempter: he offers the shortcut.
